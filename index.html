About Sushma Swaraj Institute

Our Mission

The Sushma Swaraj Institute of Foreign Service (SSIFS) is dedicated to excellence in diplomatic training, foreign policy research, and international relations education.

Training Programs

Our comprehensive training programs cover diplomatic practice, international law, trade negotiations, cultural diplomacy, and language training.

Global Partnerships

We collaborate with leading diplomatic academies, international organizations, and universities worldwide to provide our participants with global perspectives and networking opportunities.

Future Initiatives

We are expanding our digital diplomacy programs, establishing new research centers, and developing innovative training methodologies to meet the evolving challenges of international relations.
